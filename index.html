Lecturer · Researcher · Engineer (Mechanical/ Naval Architect)

Engineering complex systems through research, computation & intelligent design.

I am Md Riad Khan, a Lecturer in the Department of Naval Architecture and Offshore Engineering, Faculty of Engineering and Technology, at Bangladesh Maritime University. With more than seven years of prior industry experience, I bring together practical engineering expertise and university teaching. Alongside teaching, I am actively involved in academic research, thesis supervision, and consultancy projects.

My work spans mechanical systems, computational methods, heat transfer, autonomous systems, and data-driven approaches, with a broader interest in developing innovative and intelligent engineering solutions. My research interests also extend to robotics, autonomous systems, and underwater autonomous vehicles, where computational methods and intelligent technologies can be applied to address complex engineering challenges.
