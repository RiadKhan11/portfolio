I am Md Riad Khan, a Lecturer in the Department of Naval Architecture and Offshore Engineering at Bangladesh Maritime University. My academic and professional work connects naval architecture, computational engineering, heat transfer, autonomous systems, and data-driven methods.

I have experience in university teaching and thesis supervision, shipbuilding and port engineering, engineering consultancy, computational fluid dynamics, and research projects addressing practical maritime and industrial problems in Bangladesh.
